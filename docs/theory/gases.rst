Gas Generation
==============

Overview
--------

Thermal runaway reactions can generate substantial quantities of gas.

LiionTR supports two reduced-order gas-modeling paths.

The empirical path associates individual thermal runaway reactions with
prescribed gas-species yields. Reaction progress is converted directly into
molar generation rates and species inventories used by pressure and venting
models.

The elemental thermochemistry path instead generates elemental inventories and
uses an optional equilibrium backend to obtain gas composition and
thermodynamic properties. See :doc:`thermochemistry` for the implemented
architecture.


Gas Species
-----------

A gas species in LiionTR is represented by a name and molar mass.

For species :math:`s`, the molar mass is

.. math::

   M_s,

with units of kg/mol.

The molar mass is used when converting between molar and mass
quantities, particularly in compressible vent-flow calculations.


Gas Inventory
-------------

The gas inventory stores the mole amount of each species,

.. math::

   n_s,

where :math:`n_s` is expressed in mol.

For a mixture containing :math:`N_s` species, the total number of moles
is

.. math::

   n_{\mathrm{tot}}
   =
   \sum_s n_s.


Total Gas Mass
--------------

The total gas mass is

.. math::

   m_{\mathrm{gas}}
   =
   \sum_s n_s M_s,

where :math:`M_s` is the molar mass of species :math:`s`.

The result is expressed in kg.


Mole Fractions
--------------

The mole fraction of species :math:`s` is

.. math::

   x_s
   =
   \frac{n_s}{n_{\mathrm{tot}}}.

The mole fractions satisfy

.. math::

   \sum_s x_s = 1

when the gas inventory contains a nonzero amount of gas.


Mean Molar Mass
---------------

The mole-weighted mean molar mass of the mixture is

.. math::

   \overline{M}
   =
   \sum_s x_s M_s.

Equivalently,

.. math::

   \overline{M}
   =
   \frac{
       \sum_s n_s M_s
   }{
       \sum_s n_s
   }.

The mean molar mass is used by the current mixture vent-flow model.


Reaction Gas Yields
-------------------

The empirical gas-generation model associates each thermal runaway
reaction with one or more gas-species yields.

For species :math:`s`, the prescribed yield is

.. math::

   Y_s,

with units of mol/kg of reacted material.

If a reaction represents a mass fraction :math:`w` of the total cell
mass and progresses at a rate

.. math::

   \frac{d\alpha}{dt},

then the reacted-material mass rate is

.. math::

   \frac{dm_{\mathrm{reacted}}}{dt}
   =
   m_{\mathrm{cell}}
   w
   \frac{d\alpha}{dt}.

The corresponding gas-generation rate is

.. math::

   \dot{n}_s
   =
   Y_s
   m_{\mathrm{cell}}
   w
   \frac{d\alpha}{dt}.

The resulting unit is mol/s.


Multiple Gas Species
--------------------

One reaction may generate several gas species simultaneously.

For a reaction with species yields

.. math::

   Y_1, Y_2, \ldots, Y_m,

the corresponding generation-rate vector is

.. math::

   \dot{\boldsymbol{n}}
   =
   \left[
       \dot{n}_1,
       \dot{n}_2,
       \ldots,
       \dot{n}_m
   \right].

If multiple reactions generate the same species, their contributions
are added.


Gas Generation Network
----------------------

The gas-generation model is coupled to a
:class:`liiontr.reactions.network.ReactionNetwork`.

For each reaction, the model obtains the reaction progress rate and
applies the corresponding gas yields.

The total generation rate of species :math:`s` is therefore

.. math::

   \dot{n}_{s,\mathrm{gen}}
   =
   \sum_i
   Y_{i,s}
   m_{\mathrm{cell}}
   w_i
   \dot{\alpha}_i,

where :math:`i` indexes thermal runaway reactions.


Cumulative Generated Gas
------------------------

For a reaction that progresses from an initial conversion
:math:`\alpha_{i,0}` to a current conversion :math:`\alpha_i`, the
cumulative reacted material is proportional to

.. math::

   \Delta \alpha_i
   =
   \alpha_i
   -
   \alpha_{i,0}.

The cumulative quantity of species :math:`s` generated by that reaction
is

.. math::

   n_{i,s}
   =
   Y_{i,s}
   m_{\mathrm{cell}}
   w_i
   \left(
       \alpha_i
       -
       \alpha_{i,0}
   \right).

Summing over reactions gives

.. math::

   n_{s,\mathrm{generated}}
   =
   \sum_i n_{i,s}.


Closed-Cell Gas Balance
-----------------------

Before vent opening, generated gas remains inside the cell free volume.

For each species,

.. math::

   \frac{dn_s}{dt}
   =
   \dot{n}_{s,\mathrm{gen}}.

The gas inventory therefore increases according to the integrated
reaction-driven gas generation.


Vented Gas Balance
------------------

After vent opening, gas can both be generated and discharged.

The species balance becomes

.. math::

   \frac{dn_s}{dt}
   =
   \dot{n}_{s,\mathrm{gen}}
   -
   \dot{n}_{s,\mathrm{vent}}.

The current solver treats vent opening as irreversible: once the vent
opens, the system remains in the vented phase.


Coupling to Pressure
--------------------

The gas inventory determines the total amount of gas in the cell free
volume.

The pressure model then uses

.. math::

   n_{\mathrm{tot}}
   =
   \sum_s n_s

together with temperature and free volume to determine internal
pressure.

In the current implementation, pressure is calculated using an
ideal-gas model.

The pressure formulation is described separately in the pressure-model
documentation.


Coupling to Venting
-------------------

The gas inventory also provides the mixture composition and mean molar
mass required by the current vent-flow model.

The vent-flow model calculates a total discharge rate and distributes it
among individual species according to the gas-mixture mole fractions.

This approach assumes that the vented mixture has the same composition
as the well-mixed gas inventory inside the free volume.


Units
-----

LiionTR uses SI units internally for gas calculations.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 45 30

   * - Quantity
     - Unit

   * - Gas amount
     - mol

   * - Gas-generation rate
     - mol/s

   * - Species yield
     - mol/kg reacted material

   * - Molar mass
     - kg/mol

   * - Gas mass
     - kg

   * - Mole fraction
     - dimensionless

   * - Cell mass
     - kg

   * - Reaction mass fraction
     - dimensionless


Current Assumptions
-------------------

The empirical gas-generation framework currently assumes that:

* gas yields are prescribed directly for each reaction;
* gas yields are constant and independent of temperature and pressure;
* generated species are known in advance;
* gas-phase chemical reactions are not explicitly represented;
* equilibrium composition is not calculated;
* condensation and phase separation are neglected;
* the free-volume gas is spatially well mixed;
* the gas temperature is represented by the cell temperature in the
  coupled thermal problem;
* gas dissolution in electrolyte or porous materials is not represented.


Limitations of Direct Species Yields
------------------------------------

Direct reaction-to-species yields are useful for testing, reduced-order
models, and empirical fitting, but they have important limitations.

Actual lithium-ion battery thermal runaway may produce complex mixtures
containing species such as hydrogen, carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide,
water vapor, methane, ethylene, and other hydrocarbons.

The product distribution can depend on temperature, state of charge,
electrode chemistry, electrolyte composition, pressure, secondary
gas-phase chemistry, and material decomposition pathways.

A fixed yield model cannot generally capture these dependencies.


Elemental Thermochemistry Path
------------------------------

The empirical species-yield formulation remains available, but it is no longer
the only gas architecture in LiionTR. The implemented elemental pathway uses

.. math::

   \text{reaction/energy release}
   \rightarrow
   \text{element inventory}
   \rightarrow
   \text{equilibrium gas state}.

The optional :class:`liiontr.chemistry.cantera.CanteraEquilibriumBackend`
provides constant-``T,V`` and constant-``U,V`` equilibrium calculations.
Element-consistent venting and gas-energy accounting are implemented in the
``gases`` and ``thermal`` packages.

The default Cantera configuration uses GRI-3.0 carrier species for C/H/O/N; it
is therefore a thermochemical framework rather than a complete mechanism for
all battery-relevant F/P/Li chemistry. See :doc:`thermochemistry`.


Implementation
--------------

Gas species and inventories are implemented in

:mod:`liiontr.gases.inventory`.

Empirical reaction gas yields are implemented by

:class:`liiontr.gases.yield_model.ReactionGasYield`.

Reaction-network gas generation is implemented by

:class:`liiontr.gases.generation.GasGenerationModel`.

Pressure calculations are handled separately by

:class:`liiontr.gases.ideal.IdealGasPressureModel`.

Gas discharge is handled by the vent-flow models in

:mod:`liiontr.gases.vent`.
